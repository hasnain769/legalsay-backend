NON-DISCLOSURE AGREEMENT

This Non-Disclosure Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into as of November 1, 2025, by and between Startup Inc. ("Disclosing Party") and Investor LLC ("Receiving Party").

1. CONFIDENTIAL INFORMATION
"Confidential Information" means all non-public information disclosed by Disclosing Party.

2. OBLIGATIONS
Receiving Party agrees to hold Confidential Information in strict confidence and not to disclose it to any third party for a period of two (2) years from the date of disclosure.

3. EXCLUSIONS
Confidential Information shall not include information that is publicly known.

4. GOVERNING LAW
This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of the State of New York.
